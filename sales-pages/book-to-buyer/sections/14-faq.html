FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to have my book idea figured out before attending?

No. The Prep Session and Day 1 are specifically designed to lock in your Core Concept, ideal reader, and money-making mission before you write a single chapter. You will arrive with raw expertise and leave with a fully positioned book strategy.

What if I have already started writing my book?

Bring what you have. Most experts who arrive with drafts realize during Day 1 that their structure was the bottleneck the whole time. You may rewrite, restructure, or use existing material as raw input. The C.O.R.E. process tightens whatever you bring.

What if I am not a good writer?

Our AI-powered process is built for non-writers. You speak. The system writes. Day 2 and Day 3 use guided prompts that pull your expertise out of your head and onto the page. Eric Berman called this "next level AI prompting." You do not need to be a writer. You need to be an expert.

Can I really write an entire book in 5 days?

Yes. Every B2B graduate who followed the process completed their book in 5 days. The Prep Session locks the strategy. Days 1-3 build the outline and write the manuscript. Day 4 builds the workbook and conversion infrastructure. Day 5 publishes. By Friday night you have a finished, formatted, publication-ready book.

What if I do not have a big audience or email list yet?

Most attendees do not. Book To Buyer is designed to BUILD your list, not require one. Your book becomes the front of the funnel. The workbook captures emails. The funnel architecture turns readers into clients. You leave with the system to grow your list, not a requirement to have one.

Is travel, hotel, and food included in the price?

Catered lunches, snacks, refreshments, the Welcome Reception, and the Celebration Dinner are included. Travel to Las Vegas and your hotel stay are not. We strongly recommend staying at the ARIA for full immersion. Hotel booking instructions provided on enrollment.

What if something comes up and I cannot attend?

Once you enroll, your seat is locked and the team begins your prep work. Because of the small group size and the work that goes into your strategy before the event, we do not offer refunds. If a true emergency arises, we will work with you to transfer your seat to a future cohort.

What if I am not comfortable with AI?

You do not need to be. The AI process is woven into the workshop and guided live. By Day 2 you will be fluent in the exact prompts and workflows we use. We meet you wherever you are and walk you through it step by step.

What if I have more questions?

Email Joy directly at joy@healatscale.com. She reads and responds personally. If you are on the fence, that is the fastest way to get clarity.
