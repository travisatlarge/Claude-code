LAYER 01

The Authority Book

Your book is not a memoir. It is a positioning tool.

Every chapter is reverse-engineered from one question: What does my ideal client need to believe in order to want to work with me?

We build what I call a Content Construct: your chapter-level blueprint that maps the key teaching points. Then we turn that Content Construct into a Cash Flow Construct: a framework where every chapter has built-in "waterslides" (QR codes, lead magnets, calls to action) that invite readers to go deeper through a supportive companion workbook. No hard selling in the chapters. Just genuine invitations to implement what they are learning.

The result is not just a book. It is a book that pre-sells your backend offer on every page.

LAYER 02

The Book Funnel

Amazon is the worst place to sell your expert book. (I know. Stay with me.)

Amazon treats your book like a commodity. It puts you next to 4 million other titles. It takes your margin. And it gives you the buyer's email exactly zero percent of the time.

A book funnel flips the model. You own the customer relationship. You control the math. Your book becomes a self-liquidating front-end offer: the ad spend you put in comes back to you through order bumps, upsells, and backend conversions.

The goal is not to make money selling books. The goal is to acquire clients at breakeven (or better) so you can serve them on the backend with your coaching, programs, and services. That is where the real revenue lives.

LAYER 03

The Authority Ecosystem

This is the layer nobody else builds. And it is the reason Book To Buyer graduates do not just publish a book. They build a platform.

Your book content does not live in the book alone. The same frameworks, stories, and teaching become: workbook content that fills your list. Video and podcast material for 12+ months. Social media content. Email nurture sequences. Speaking material. Client onboarding resources.

One book. Written once. Deployed across every revenue channel in your business.

This is not repurposing. This is architecture. You build the book with the ecosystem in mind from Day 1. That is why we map it all during B2B LIVE, not after.
