The $10,000 Text Message That Changed Sherry's Practice.

Dr. Sherry Brewer swore she would never write a book. Five days later, she had one. Here's her story,

I never thought I could write a book. I'm not a great writer. I'm analytical, more at home with data and charts than words. But I could talk anyone's ear off about functional medicine.

That was my problem. I knew my stuff, but I had no organized framework to describe what I do. So, I couldn't articulate it well on sales calls. And because I'd see anyone who wanted a functional medicine doctor, my clinic was full of patients with conditions I didn't particularly enjoy treating. I wanted a book that would give my process a framework and give me street cred with patients and peers. I also wanted it to get me on podcasts and stages.

Going in, I was scared. I didn't know how to organize it or what chapters to include. Then I got to Book To Buyer with Joy and Travis, and it was a blast.

They had the entire process laid out. We did the prep work mapping my process and gathering patient stories. Then we spent five days hunkered down at a fabulous hotel with other authors. I spent 20 hour speaking my content and never had to put pen to paper.

They literally pulled the book out of me. By day five I had my first draft in the book writing software, ISBN numbers, an author bio, KDP steps, and marketing tools like a workbook outline and a process to build a book funnel.

When I hit publish on Why You Can't Lose Weight, I filmed it and sent it to Joy and Travis. They were at a restaurant and filmed me right back, hooting and hollering. It made me cry. I hit #1 best seller, something I never dreamed I could do.

Since then, people I hadn't heard from in 40 years have reconnected. Patients sign up for 1:1 care just knowing I have a book, even before reading it. It feeds traction to my precision gut test. I've lectured and been on several podcasts. Best of all, it brings me the exact clients I want, and they tell me it felt like I wrote it for them.

If you're a busy practitioner on the fence: the process is rock solid. Give yourself five days. You won't regret it.

Dr. Sherry Brewer, Functional Medicine Practitioner and Author
